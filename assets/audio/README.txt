QUIET RELAY

An original 80-second atmospheric study cue, composed with the user-invoked
Midify plugin. The complete ABC score was accepted by Midify without warnings.
Midify provides composition/playback, but its installed tool does not export
audio. quiet-relay.wav is an offline synthesized rendition of the same notes,
durations, tempo and four parts, not an audio export from Midify.

The score evokes a quiet, melancholy science-fiction atmosphere. It does not
contain melodies, recordings, samples or lyrics from the NieR soundtrack.

Audio format: stereo PCM WAV, 22050 Hz, 16 bits. Duration: 80 seconds.
Music loops with a soft fade at the seam. UI effects are synthesized locally
by audio.js and require no separate downloads or network access.

Music and effects each begin off. Turn them on using the game's sound controls.
